FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Experience Modernization?

The process of rebuilding a web page to deliver the same visual story with dramatically better performance. The goal is not changing the content  it is making the same content feel instant.

Will the page look different after modernization?

No. The visual intent stays the same. Hero headings, stats, offer cards, FAQ content, and CTAs all survive the migration. Only the underlying implementation changes.

How much faster will the modernized page be?

Typical improvements move a page from a "Poor" Core Web Vitals score to "Good"  often reducing LCP by 6080% and eliminating layout shifts entirely.

What content is preserved during migration?

All visible content: hero heading, text, CTAs, stats, offer cards with images and copy, FAQ items, and the final CTA section.

Do I need to re-author content after modernization?

No. Content is migrated as-is. Authors can continue editing through the standard workflow without any re-training.
