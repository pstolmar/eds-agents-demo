FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Experience Modernization?

Experience Modernization is the process of rebuilding a web page to deliver the same visual story with dramatically better performance. The goal is not changing the content  it is making the same content feel instant.

Will the page look different after modernization?

No. The visual intent stays the same. Hero headings, stats, offer cards, FAQ content, and CTAs all survive the migration. Only the underlying implementation changes to remove render-blocking work and heavy DOM generation.

How much faster will the modernized page be?

Typical improvements move a page from a "Poor" Core Web Vitals score to "Good"  often reducing Largest Contentful Paint by 6080% and eliminating layout shifts entirely.

What content is preserved during migration?

All visible content is preserved: hero heading, descriptive text, call-to-action buttons, statistics, offer cards with images and copy, FAQ accordion items, and the final CTA section.

Do I need to re-author content after modernization?

No. Content is migrated as-is into AEM Edge Delivery Services blocks. Authors can continue editing through the standard document-based authoring workflow without any re-training.
